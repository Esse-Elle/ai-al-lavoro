Chapter eleven, part two. On costs: every generation uses tokens, depending on model, resolution and number of images, and videos cost much more. The free plan gives a hundred and fifty tokens a day, and paid plans range from about twelve to about sixty dollars a month. A few tips to avoid wasting them: generate one or two images at a time, explore at low resolution, only upscale the best ones and only animate the images you've chosen.

The last pipeline goes from idea to video. Claude turns the brief into a concept and writes the scene prompts. Leonardo generates the images with the right model, a few at a time. Then it retouches them with words and upscales the best ones. It animates them with Kling or Veo. And finally Claude, with HyperFrames, takes care of editing, music and brand checks. The right images first, then the movement: video costs many more tokens.

And a few things to watch out for. Real products don't come out accurately: use them as a reference, or do compositing. Text in images is often wrong: add it afterwards, or put it in with Claude Design, as vector or in InDesign, or with ChatGPT. Check the plan's terms for commercial use. Guide palette, light and materials to stay consistent with the brand. And when needed, state that a piece of content was generated with AI.
