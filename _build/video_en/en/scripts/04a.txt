Chapter four: let's go inside Claude. To find our way around, picture a map with eight building blocks. The chat, where you talk and reason. Projects, your collaborator's memory. Artifacts, in other words the finished work. Scheduled tasks, which work on their own at set times. Personalisation, so Claude knows who you are and how you write. Connectors, the sockets to your tools. Claude Design, the in-house graphic designer. And Claude Code, the tool builder. You don't need to use them all straight away: you start with chat and projects, and add the rest when you need it.

Where do you use Claude? The same Claude lives in several places. There's the desktop app, for Mac and Windows, which is the full version and the one I recommend. There's the browser, on claude.ai, handy from any computer. There's the phone app, to chat, dictate and follow tasks in progress. There's Claude in Chrome, an extension that browses and acts on websites. There are Claude in Excel and in PowerPoint, which work inside Office files, and Claude for Google Workspace, in Docs, Sheets and Slides. Your account, projects, artifacts and scheduled tasks are the same everywhere.

So, desktop or browser? For chatting, both are fine. The difference is something else: the desktop app is the gateway to your computer. Folders, the built-in browser and computer control only work while the desktop app is open, even if at that moment you're working from the browser or your phone. Without the desktop app, Claude can't see your folders. That's why my first piece of advice is always the same: install it.

Now the latest change. Until recently, when you opened Claude you had to choose between Chat and Cowork. Today that choice is gone: there's one single conversation. You just write. If you ask for an idea, Claude answers. If you ask for a piece of work, for example research, a report, a spreadsheet or a presentation, it gets to work in the same conversation. In Recents you'll find chats and tasks together. There's no web search switch any more: Claude searches when it needs to. For in-depth research you type "slash deep research", or choose Research from the plus button. Projects work in every conversation. And only the Code tab stays separate.

Where did things end up? General instructions are in Settings, General, Instructions for Claude. Authorised folders are in Settings, under Trusted folders. Permissions are chosen right in the box where you type: Manual or Auto. You'll find your old tasks in Recents. And for files there's an important guarantee: Claude always asks before permanently deleting anything.

Let's take a tour of the workspace. On the left there's the sidebar with Recents, Projects, Artifacts, Scheduled and Customize. In the middle, the conversation, with your requests and the answers. Every now and then an approval request appears, with two buttons: Allow or Deny. At the bottom, the input box: the plus to attach files or start research, the slash to call up a skill, and next to it the choice of model, effort and permissions. On the right, while Claude works, you see the progress step by step, the context it's using, meaning folders, connectors and skills, and the outputs, the files it created, ready to open with a click.
