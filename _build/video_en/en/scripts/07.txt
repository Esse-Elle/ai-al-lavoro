Chapter seven: the ideal setup, in ten moves. One: install the desktop app and sign in with your company account. Two: in Settings, General, write your Instructions for Claude. Three: set Sonnet with medium effort as the default. Four: authorise your work folder among the trusted folders. Five: create the folder and the linked project. Six: upload the brand book, tone of voice and approved examples. Seven: install the skills for Word, Excel and PowerPoint and the Marketing plugin. Eight: connect only the useful connectors, involving IT. Nine: permissions on Manual. Ten: once a month, take a look at memory and usage.

An example of personal instructions, to adapt: "I work in marketing at the Colombini Group, in the premium furniture sector. When data is missing, ask me for it, don't make it up. For long texts, propose an outline first. Always cite your sources when you search the web. If a task is ambiguous, ask me a question before you start." These instructions apply in every conversation, and projects add their specific rules.

A few words on memory and privacy. Claude remembers facts and preferences from one conversation to the next. You can see this memory, correct it and delete it in the settings, and in projects it's separate: each project has its own. On privacy: use your company account, not your personal one, and don't upload sensitive customer data unless you need to.

And finally, the mistakes to avoid, which we've all seen happen. Endless chats on different topics, which waste tokens and cause confusion. Uploading the same files every time instead of using the folder. Using the most powerful model for everything. Touching up a file by hand and then asking Claude to regenerate it. Giving access to the whole disk or to every account. Trusting numbers and facts without checking them. And starting with the most complex automation.
