Chapter nine: trust, and the first step. Always check. With images and texts generated by AI, check rights, trademarks and consistency with the guidelines. Real products must not be invented by AI. And numbers and data must always be double-checked. The verification method has four moves: ask for sources, compare with data you know, use your expert judgment, and test small before you publish. Let me repeat the sentence from the beginning: AI empowers the people who use it, it doesn't replace judgment.
